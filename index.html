Fit / not fit

Substance and participation are required.

The system amplifies real expertise. It does not manufacture it.

You’re likely a fit if…

  • You run an existing business and have credible expertise or customer evidence.
  • You want to understand practical AI rather than outsource your thinking.
  • You can attend weekly working sessions and give consolidated feedback.
  • You are willing to record, approve, and consistently publish.

This is not a fit if…

  • You need AI to invent expertise, opinions, or proof you do not have.
  • You want fully managed production, posting, or audience management.
  • You expect guaranteed views, followers, leads, revenue, or virality.
  • You are not prepared to participate in or publish the work.
Scope boundaries: Creator Lab covers strategy, practical AI education, system building, and content pre-production. There is no filming, editing, finished thumbnails, account management, or guaranteed performance.
