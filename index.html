Qualification

You’re likely a fit if…

  • ✓You run an existing business and have credible expertise or customer evidence.
  • ✓You want to understand practical AI—not outsource your thinking to it.
  • ✓You can join a weekly private session and give clear, consolidated feedback.

This is not a fit if…

  • ×You need AI to invent expertise, opinions, or customer proof you do not have.

  • ×You are not prepared to publish or participate in the process.
Honest scope: Creator Lab Private covers strategy, AI education, system building, and content pre-production. It does not include custom software development, filming, video editing, thumbnail production, posting, audience management, sales execution, or performance guarantees.
FAQ

Clear answers before you apply.

It is a done-with-you partnership. You learn practical AI while we build your personalized Creator OS together. Strategy, system design, Claude configuration, and content pre-production are included; your participation, recording, final approval, and publishing are essential.

No advanced experience is required. We focus on the practical concepts and habits needed to operate your workspace and Skills confidently—not technical complexity for its own sake.

Each package covers the premise, viewer promise, title directions, thumbnail concepts, research, proof requirements, and an outline or script. You or your production partner handles filming, editing, final thumbnail production, upload, and publishing.

Plan for a weekly private working session, plus time to share source material, test the system, provide consolidated feedback, record, approve, and publish. The exact workload depends on your existing content process.

No views, followers, leads, revenue, or virality are guaranteed. The scope is the agreed strategy, education, system-building work, content assets, and handoff. Market response depends on many factors outside the partnership.

You receive the final playbook and handoff so you can continue operating the Creator OS. Any future support would be discussed separately based on what the first 90 days reveal.
