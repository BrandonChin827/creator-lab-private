How it works

Here's how the 90 days actually work.

No rushed prompt dump. Everything runs on one loop — Capture, Build, Publish, Improve — and we install it in three deliberate phases.

Days 1–30

Extract & architect

We start with what you already know. Together we clarify your positioning, capture your voice, stories, frameworks, and proof, and organize it all into a structured knowledge base. Then we configure your personalized Claude workspace around it — so from day one, the AI is grounded in your actual expertise, not generic internet filler.

Days 31–60

Operate

This is where it becomes real. We build and test your personalized Claude Skills, then run actual YouTube pre-production and X assets through the system every week. You review, approve, and publish. Each weekly session sharpens the workflow around how you really work.

Days 61–90

Transfer & improve

We refine the system around real response signals, assign ownership, and document everything into an operating playbook. By day 90, you can run the Creator OS without us.
