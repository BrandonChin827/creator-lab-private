Privacy Policy

Last updated September 30, 2026

Portlock Creative ("we") runs portlockcreative.com. This page explains, in plain language, what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • What you type into our forms: your name and email, plus any optional answers (like whether you have a YouTube channel, what you do, and your channel link).
  • Booking details: if you book a call, the answers you give in our booking form and scheduling tool.
  • How you found us: the link or video that sent you to our site (for example, tracking tags in a link).

How we use it

  • To send you what you asked for, like the free skill.
  • To email you about growing on YouTube and about our services. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • To understand which videos and content bring people to us.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information. We use a few trusted services to run the site and they handle your data on our behalf: Kit (email), Vercel (website hosting), Tally and Calendly (booking a call), and ClickLedger (seeing which videos send visitors).

Your choices

  • Unsubscribe any time using the link at the bottom of any email.
  • Email brandon@portlockcreative.com to see, correct, or delete the information we have about you.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

Questions? Email brandon@portlockcreative.com.
